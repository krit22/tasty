Implemented `logsum` in `main.go` with validation, deterministic ordering, 1 MiB record support, and continued processing after unreadable entries.

Tests cover invalid records, large lines, ordering, argument errors, and I/O failures. Tests, `go vet`, build, and smoke test passed.

Build and run:

```sh
go build -buildvcs=false -o logsum .
./logsum DIRECTORY
```
